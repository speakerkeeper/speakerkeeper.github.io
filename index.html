The problem

You pause for a minute. It decides you're done.

Almost every Bluetooth speaker powers itself off when no sound comes in — on purpose, to save battery. Great for a speaker in a bag. Annoying for the one on your desk.

01

The first second vanishes

You press play and the opening of the track is clipped, because the speaker is still waking up.

02

Nothing plays at all

Sometimes it's gone far enough to sleep that you have to reconnect it by hand before anything comes out.

03

It disappears from Windows

It drops off your sound settings entirely until you walk over and press its power button again.

How it works

It plays nothing, on purpose.

Not a faint hum, not an inaudible tone — true digital silence, where every sample is a zero. Your volume slider makes no difference, because there is no sound to make louder.

STEP 01

It finds your speaker

Speaker Keeper watches your default audio output and follows along when you switch devices.

STEP 02

It streams silence

A silent media session keeps the audio path busy, so the speaker believes something is playing.

STEP 03

Your speaker stays on

No idle timeout, no wake-up delay, no reconnecting. It behaves like it's simply always on.

Features

Small, quiet, and out of the way.

It sits in your system tray and asks nothing of you.

Truly silent

Digital silence, not a faint hum or a tone. Nothing comes out of the speaker.

Shows your battery

Hover the tray icon to see your speaker's charge, and get a warning when it runs low.

Leaves earbuds alone

Only works on Bluetooth speakers. It won't hold your earbuds awake and drain them.

Your media keys still work

Play/pause and next-track keep controlling Spotify, YouTube and everything else.

Follows your speaker

Switch audio output and it comes along automatically. Nothing to reconfigure.

Tiny and private

One small app. No account, no background service, no telemetry of any kind.
