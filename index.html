The problem

You pause for a minute. It decides you're done.

Almost every Bluetooth speaker powers itself off when no sound comes in, on purpose, to save battery. Great for a Bluetooth speaker in a bag. Annoying for the one on your desk.

01

The first second vanishes

You press play and the opening of the track is clipped, because the Bluetooth speaker is still waking up.

02

Nothing plays at all

Sometimes it's gone far enough to sleep that you have to reconnect it by hand before anything comes out.

03

It disappears from Windows

It drops off your sound settings entirely until you walk over and press its power button again.

What changes

Your Bluetooth speaker starts behaving like it's wired.

Same Bluetooth speaker, same connection, none of the waiting. You stop thinking about it entirely, which is the whole idea.

Features

Free, private, and out of your way.

Set it up once. It looks after itself from there.

All of your Bluetooth speakers

Set it up for each Bluetooth speaker you own. It remembers them, so you can move between the one on your desk and the one in the kitchen without touching a thing.

Everything in one click

See which Bluetooth speaker you're using, how much battery it has left, and turn it off for that Bluetooth speaker. It warns you when the battery gets low.

Leaves earbuds alone

It only works on Bluetooth speakers. Your earbuds and headphones still switch themselves off when you put them away.

Free, lifetime updates included

Small and private

FAQ

Questions worth asking.

Install Speaker Keeper and leave it running. It holds a silent audio stream open on the Bluetooth speaker you choose, so it never sees the silence that makes it power down and Windows keeps the connection up.

Nothing is audible and nothing changes on your PC. It sits in the tray, uses almost nothing, and there is no setting to get right.

Leave your Bluetooth speaker plugged in. Speaker Keeper is built for the Bluetooth speaker that lives on your desk, and one sitting on its charger is simply always ready, which is the setup it's happiest in.

Away from a charger it works just as well, and keeps an eye on the battery for you: it stays awake rather than asleep, so it uses power the way it does while you're listening, and it tells you in good time when it's worth plugging in.

A macOS version is on our roadmap. First we're launching Speaker Keeper on the Microsoft Store, and a Mac build comes after that.
